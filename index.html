HARIKA

A personal assistant for your Windows computer. Ask in your own words, typed or spoken, and HARIKA opens apps, finds files, sets reminders, checks your calendar and email, answers questions from the live web, and works through bigger tasks with you.

Download for Windows

What it does

  • Opens and switches apps, controls volume, brightness, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and dark mode.
  • Reminders, timers, notes, lists, routines and a morning briefing.
  • Google Calendar and Gmail: reads your day and email, and drafts replies that are sent only when you say yes.
  • Reads the app you're using, helps you reply in chats, and tells you when an app finishes.
  • Answers about news and current events with sources, and researches and compares for you.

Your privacy

HARIKA runs on your computer and has no servers of its own. It uses the AI service you choose, or a model on your own computer. Anything that changes something or reaches other people waits for your yes. Read the privacy policy.
