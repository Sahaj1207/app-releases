HARIKA

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

HARIKA is a personal assistant app that you install on your own Windows computer. This policy explains what information HARIKA uses, where it goes, and how you stay in control. HARIKA has no servers of its own: the people who make HARIKA never receive your conversations, files, calendar or email.

Google Calendar and Gmail

If you choose Sign in with Google in HARIKA, Google asks you to allow these permissions. HARIKA uses each one only to do what you ask it to:

  • Your email address (openid, email): to show which Google account is connected.
  • Calendar events (calendar.events): to tell you what's on your calendar, add events you ask for, and move or delete an event. Moving or deleting always waits for your explicit "yes". If you turn on the morning briefing or meeting heads-ups, HARIKA reads today's and upcoming events for them.
  • Reading Gmail (gmail.readonly): to list new emails, search for emails you ask about, summarize an email you ask HARIKA to read, find a person's address from your past emails when you ask HARIKA to email them, and count new emails for your morning briefing if it's on.
  • Sending Gmail (gmail.send): to send an email or reply only after you have seen the exact draft and said "yes".

HARIKA does not change, delete, label or archive your emails.

Where your Google data goes

  • HARIKA talks to Google directly from your computer. Your Google sign-in token is stored in Windows Credential Manager on your computer.
  • To understand your request and write an answer, a summary or a draft, the relevant text (for example the email you asked HARIKA to summarize, or the titles and times of your events) is sent to the AI service you choose in HARIKA's Settings: a model running on your own computer, or a cloud service such as Groq or Google Gemini, under that service's own terms. You can choose a model on your computer if you don't want any text sent to a cloud service.
  • HARIKA does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models, and does not share it with anyone else.

HARIKA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other information HARIKA uses

  • Your requests and conversations are sent to the AI service you choose, as above. Your conversation history, memories, reminders, notes, lists, routines and the people you tell HARIKA about are stored on your computer.
  • Your screen: only when you ask about the app you are using (or ask HARIKA to watch it), HARIKA reads that window's text. It may capture that window as an image to read it; the image isn't saved. The text, or the image when you ask how something looks, is sent to your chosen AI service. You can turn image capture off in Settings > Privacy.
  • Your voice: speech recognition runs on your computer. If you connect an online voice (Azure AI Speech), the text of HARIKA's replies is sent to it to be spoken.
  • The web: to answer questions about current events, HARIKA searches the web (DuckDuckGo) and reads news headlines (Google News). For the weather it uses Open-Meteo, and, if you haven't set a home city, an approximate location from your internet address (ipwho.is).
  • Messages: when you ask HARIKA to text someone, it opens WhatsApp on your computer with the message, and sends it only after your "yes".
  • Updates: HARIKA checks this website for new versions. That request contains no personal information.

Your control

  • Disconnect Google any time in HARIKA's Settings > Accounts > Disconnect, which also revokes HARIKA's access at Google. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Your HARIKA data is in %APPDATA%\com.harika.desktop on your computer. Uninstalling HARIKA and deleting that folder removes it.
  • HARIKA is not intended for children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions or requests: open an issue.
